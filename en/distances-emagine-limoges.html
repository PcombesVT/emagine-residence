Getting around from Isle

Several transport options make it easy to reach the campuses and the city centre of Limoges from Isle.

🚶

On foot

The Faculty of Medicine & Pharmacy and the CHU Dupuytren are only a few hundred metres away. Most health students reach their place of study on foot in 5 to 10 minutes. The Faculty of Arts & Humanities is about a 13-minute walk.

🚌

STCL bus

The STCL network (Limoges public transport) serves Isle with several stops near the residence. The lines reach the city centre, the station and the other campuses in 15 to 25 minutes.

Isle line Nearby stops
🚗

Car & parking

The E-Magine residence has a numbered private parking space allocated to each studio. Isle is well connected to Limoges via the D941 and the ring road. The city centre is 8 minutes away by car, the La Borie campus 12 minutes.

🚲

Cycling & active travel

The quiet setting of Isle is especially well suited to cycling. The Faculty of Arts & Humanities (1.1 km) is under 5 minutes away by bike. The CHU, the Faculty of Medicine and the IFSI are also easy to reach.

🛵

Scooter & moped

Small vehicles (electric scooters, mopeds) are a handy alternative for longer trips to the La Borie campus or the Faculty of Law, while avoiding parking hassles in the city centre.

🚆

Bénédictins station

Limoges-Bénédictins station (a listed monument, one of the finest in France) is 20 minutes away by bus or 10 minutes by car. It offers connections to Bordeaux, Paris and Clermont-Ferrand.
