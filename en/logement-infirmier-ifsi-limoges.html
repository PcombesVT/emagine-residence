The residence closest to the IFSI and the CHU

The IFSI nursing school of the CHU de Limoges is at 39F rue Camille Guérin, about 1.1 km from the E-Magine residence (18 avenue de la République, Isle 87170). The walk follows the river Vienne and takes about 14 minutes — direct access with no need for public transport.

Letting is handled by Begip, an estate agency in Limoges specialising in student housing.

Nursing training in Limoges

The CHU de Limoges nursing school: the 3-year DEI degree

The nursing school of the CHU de Limoges prepares students for the State Nursing Diploma (DEI), which carries bachelor's-degree status (3 years). Here's what you should know about the course before choosing your home.

A 3-year programme

  • YEAR 1
    Nursing fundamentals Theory classes at the IFSI (39F rue Camille Guérin) + first observation and basic-care placements at the CHU and in care homes. Immersion in the profession from year one.
  • YEAR 2
    Clinical deepening Placements in specialist CHU wards (internal medicine, surgery, A&E, intensive care…). Half of the training time is spent on placement. Being close to the CHU from E-Magine is decisive.
  • YEAR 3
    Professionalisation & long placement A 10-week reference nursing placement in a ward of your choice. Defence of the final-year dissertation. Award of the State Nursing Diploma.

Key facts about the course

  • 📅
    Length 3 years — 4,200 hours of training (theory classes + alternating placements)
  • 🎓
    Diploma State Nursing Diploma (DEI) — bachelor level. Progression possible to an Advanced Practice Nursing (IPA) master's.
  • 🏥
    Placement sites CHU Dupuytren, care homes, home hospitalisation, mental-health units, community mental-health centres, partner private clinics.
  • 📍
    IFSI address 39F rue Camille Guérin, 87000 Limoges — the same street as ILFOMER, a ~14-min walk from E-Magine.

Frequently asked questions

FAQ — IFSI nursing student housing in Limoges

The questions nursing students ask before choosing their home in Limoges.

How far is E-Magine from the IFSI nursing school of the CHU de Limoges?

▼
The E-Magine residence (18 avenue de la République, 87170 Isle) is about 1.1 km from the IFSI (39F rue Camille Guérin, 87000 Limoges), a ~14-minute walk along the Vienne. The CHU Dupuytren, the main placement site, is about a 13-minute walk away.

Is my E-Magine rent eligible for ALS for the DEI nursing degree?

▼
Yes. The E-Magine studios are eligible for ALS (French housing benefit) paid by the CAF. As a DEI nursing student, you can apply from the moment you move in. The amount depends on your personal situation. The base rent starts at €360/month all charges included.

Does the residence suit the irregular hours of nursing placements?

▼
Yes. The E-Magine residence has 24/7 controlled access and secure private parking for returns by car after a night shift. Being close to the CHU (~13-min walk, under 5 min by bike) often means you don't need transport at all. The studios are fully furnished and equipped for quality rest between two placement days.

How do I contact Begip to rent a studio at E-Magine?

▼
Letting is handled by Begip, an estate agency in Limoges. You can reach them by phone on +33 5 55 33 97 50, by email at agence@begip.fr, or via the contact form on this site. The residence may be full — if so, ask to be added to the waiting list. We recommend contacting Begip as early as June for the September intake.
