The neighbourhood & daily life

Everything you need, within reach

Isle offers a calm, safe environment with all the essentials right next to the E-Magine residence.

🛒

Local shops

A supermarket, a pharmacy, a bakery and several local shops are within 200 m of the residence. You don't need a car for your everyday shopping.

🌿

A quiet, green setting

Isle sits on the river Vienne and is lined with green spaces. A peaceful environment, away from the bustle of the city centre, ideal for focus and rest — essential for demanding studies such as medicine.

🚌

Public transport

Several STCL bus stops (the Limoges Mobilité network) serve Isle and reach Limoges city centre, the Bénédictins railway station and the secondary campuses in 15 to 25 minutes.

🏊

Sport & leisure

Limoges has plenty of sports facilities (swimming pools, gyms, outdoor pitches) accessible from Isle. The Vienne valley also offers superb walking and cycling routes.

🍽️

Eating out

Several restaurants and pizzerias are within reach in Isle. The medical campus has a university canteen (RU). Limoges city centre, with a varied food scene, is 15 minutes away.

🔒

Safety & peace of mind

Isle is a residential town known for its tranquillity. The E-Magine residence has secure access and private parking. Ideal for students living on their own for the first time.
