Health hub — Marcland Campus

Faculty of Medicine & Pharmacy

The Marcland Campus is home to the Faculty of Medicine and Pharmacy of the University of Limoges. About 12 minutes on foot (under 5 min by bike) from the E-Magine residence, crossing the bridge over the Vienne.

University of Limoges
Faculty of Medicine & Pharmacy
🩺

The Faculty of Medicine & Pharmacy of Limoges trains future doctors, pharmacists, midwives and health professionals. Attached to the CHU Dupuytren, it offers PASS (the specific health-access pathway), the LAS routes, medical studies from the 2nd to the 6th cycle, and the pharmacy and midwifery tracks. Its simulation centre and research laboratories make it a nationally recognised institution.

📍Marcland Campus — 2 rue du Docteur Marcland, 87025 Limoges
🎓PASS · LAS · Medicine (2nd–6th cycle) · Pharmacy · Midwifery
🌐medecine.unilim.fr
📍 ~12 min walk from E-Magine
First year — Health access
PASS / LAS — Specific Health-Access Pathway
📋

PASS (the specific health-access pathway) and LAS (health-access degrees) are the gateway to studies in medicine, pharmacy, dentistry and midwifery (the MMOPK tracks). First-year students are based on the Marcland Campus. The E-Magine residence is the ideal home for this decisive year, under the best conditions: quiet, close by and budget-friendly.

📍Marcland Campus, 87025 Limoges
🎓PASS (year 1) · LAS across various degrees
ℹ️Formerly PACES (the common first year of health studies)
📍 ~12 min walk from E-Magine
Teaching hospital — Isle & Limoges

CHU de Limoges — Dupuytren

The Limoges University Hospital is the main healthcare institution in the Limousin. Each year it welcomes hundreds of students on placement, residents and junior doctors across every specialty.

Teaching hospital
CHU de Limoges — Dupuytren Hospital
🏥

The CHU Dupuytren is a leading hospital that welcomes medical students for their clinical placements from the 4th year onwards. Residents and junior consultants complete their practical training there across more than 40 disciplines. As the reference centre for the northern Nouvelle-Aquitaine health region, it offers regionally rare specialties (neurosurgery, onco-haematology, transplantation).

📍2 avenue Martin Luther King, 87042 Limoges
🎓4th–6th-year medical placements · Residency · Junior consultancy
🌐chu-limoges.fr
📍 ~13 min walk from E-Magine

Paramedical training — Marcland / Vanteaux

Nursing, Physiotherapy & Midwifery

The CHU de Limoges paramedical training institutes are clustered around the Marcland Campus and the Vanteaux area — an easy walk from the E-Magine residence.

Paramedical training — CHU Limoges
IFSI — Nursing School
💉

The IFSI of the CHU de Limoges prepares students for the State Nursing Diploma (DEI) over 3 years. Training alternates between theory and clinical placements within the CHU and partner facilities (care homes, home hospitalisation, mental-health units). The diploma carries bachelor's-degree status (3 years). The IFSI is one of the most sought-after courses for students looking for housing near the CHU.

📍39F rue Camille Guérin, 87000 Limoges
🎓State Nursing Diploma (DEI) — 3 years (bachelor level)
ℹ️Possible progression to the advanced-practice nursing (IPA) track
📍 ~14 min walk from E-Magine

Paramedical training — CHU Limoges
ILFOMER — Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation
🦵

ILFOMER (the Limousin institute for rehabilitation and re-education professions) offers training in physiotherapy (5 years, master level), occupational therapy, podiatry and speech therapy. These highly selective courses draw students from all over France. The E-Magine residence is a stable, nearby housing solution.

📍39F rue Camille Guérin, 87000 Limoges
🎓Physiotherapist (MKDE) · Occupational therapist · Speech therapy
📍 ~14 min walk from E-Magine
University of Limoges — Health
Midwifery
👶

The midwifery track at the University of Limoges trains future midwives over 5 years after PASS. Training takes place on the Marcland Campus and within the CHU Dupuytren (obstetrics and gynaecology department). Midwifery students often look for reliable, affordable housing right by the campus — exactly what E-Magine offers.

📍Marcland Campus, 87025 Limoges (training attached to the CHU)
🎓State Diploma in Midwifery — 5 years (master level)
📍 ~12 min walk from E-Magine
Arts, Languages & Humanities — Vanteaux Campus

Faculty of Arts & Humanities

The Faculty of Arts & Humanities is at 39E rue Camille Guérin, just 1.1 km from the E-Magine residence — about ten minutes on foot or a few minutes by bike. To learn more about this green district in south-west Limoges, its shops and its atmosphere, see the Vanteaux neighbourhood guide.

University of Limoges
Faculty of Arts & Humanities
📚

The Faculty of Arts & Humanities of the University of Limoges offers programmes from bachelor's to doctorate in modern letters, applied foreign languages (LEA), history, geography, philosophy, psychology, education sciences and information & communication. Its master's in museology is recognised nationally. These courses attract students from all over the world, who find at E-Magine an ideal base.

📍39E rue Camille Guérin, 87000 Limoges
🎓Bachelor · Master · Doctorate — Letters, Languages, History, Psychology, Geography
🌐flsh.unilim.fr
📍 1.1 km — ~13 min walk from E-Magine
Grande École — Art & Design
ENSAD — National School of Art and Design
🎨

Based on the Vanteaux campus, ENSAD Limoges is world-renowned for its training in ceramic design (a legacy of Limoges porcelain), object design and contemporary art. The course runs for 5 years (DNA at year 3, then DNSEP at year 5). Its students come from around the world and look for affordable, well-located housing.

📍Vanteaux Campus, Avenue Martin Luther King, 87000 Limoges
🎓DNA (year 3) · DNSEP (year 5) — Design & Art
📍 ~12–15 min walk from E-Magine
Other nearby programmes

KEYCE Santé & private schools

Other health and paramedical institutions are within reach of the E-Magine residence, including private schools.

Private school — Health
KEYCE Santé Limoges
⚕️

KEYCE Santé offers private courses preparing for health entrance exams (nursing, physiotherapy, etc.), as well as osteopathy and health management. The school is at 59-61 rue Bobillot in Limoges. For KEYCE students looking for student housing in Limoges, the E-Magine residence offers furnished studios a few minutes away by public transport — an ideal solution from the first year.

Faculty of Law & Economics
⚖️
