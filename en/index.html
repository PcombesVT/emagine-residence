Student life

Studying medicine in Limoges

A human-scale city, a leading teaching hospital, and an outstanding setting for your studies.

🏥

A leading teaching hospital

The CHU of Limoges is recognised for the excellence of its departments and welcomes hundreds of students on placement across every specialty each year.

🎓

High-quality training

The Faculty of Medicine at the University of Limoges is known for its well-regarded teaching, with a simulation centre and advanced research laboratories.

🌿

A pleasant place to live

Isle offers a quiet, green environment at the gates of Limoges, ideal for demanding studies. The cost of living here is among the lowest in France.
